## Work(s) covered

The undersigned participants request that BMI revise the registration data for
the works listed in the attached **BMI Correction Matrix**. Each work is
identified by its BMI work number.

This letter is limited to correcting BMI's catalog information. It does not
create a new work, transfer a master recording, or change any right except the
writer/composer, publisher, title, artist or share information expressly shown
as corrected in the attached matrix.

## Direction to BMI

We jointly instruct BMI to use the corrected information in the matrix and the
supporting documents attached for the requested update. If a split sheet is
provided for a work, it is included as supporting documentation.
We confirm that:

- the corrected writer/composer information is accurate;
- the corrected publisher information is accurate, if a publisher is listed;
- the corrected writer shares total the appropriate writer-share amount;
- the corrected publisher shares total the appropriate publisher-share amount,
  if publisher shares are assigned;
- each signatory has reviewed the proposed correction for the affected work(s);
- BMI may contact any signatory if further confirmation is required.

If any requested change removes a participant or reduces a participant's share,
that affected participant must sign below or provide separate written consent
attached to this letter.
